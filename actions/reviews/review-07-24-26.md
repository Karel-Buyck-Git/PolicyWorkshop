## New findings

- **Onboarding skill is out of sync with the schema (`#42`).** 27c retired `hierarchyVersion` from
  `expand.py`, both schemas, the template and contoso — but
  `skills/epac-builder-onboarding-agent/SKILL.md:167,188` still tells the interview to collect
  `source.hierarchyVersion`. `manifest.schema.json` sets `additionalProperties: false`, so I
  confirmed a manifest authored per those instructions **fails validation**:
  `$.source.hierarchyVersion: unexpected property`. This breaks one of the three invocation surfaces
  today; fix is two lines.
- **The stamp check I just ran by hand should be a CI step (`#43`).** Recomputing the 8 provenance
  hashes with the existing `shared/hashing.py` is ~20 lines and seconds of runtime — no producer
  re-run, no policy-source fetch. It would have caught the 07-23 stale-catalogue drift on the day it
  happened. A cheap slice of #8 that delivers most of its protection at a fraction of the effort.
- **#21's code path has zero regression coverage (`#44`).** Contoso selects api-management + tags —
  neither declares `customerAbbreviation`, so the *only* initiative exercising the new bubbled
  initiative-level-parameter path is untested by CI. The naming build I ran to verify #21 was ad hoc
  and isn't committed anywhere.
- **No `.gitattributes` (`#45`).** The whole byte-diff contract currently rests on every contributor
  having `core.autocrlf=true` (this machine does; fixtures are LF in the index). A Windows
  contributor with `autocrlf=false` commits CRLF fixtures and Linux CI fails the byte-diff for a
  non-reason.
