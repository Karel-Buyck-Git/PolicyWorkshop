# Company Web Enterprise — App Service

## Tier rationale

**Enterprise** — Zero-trust and regulatory alignment for App Service: controls that require infrastructure investment or map directly to compliance frameworks. This tier delivers private endpoints and private link removing the public attack surface and diagnostic settings streaming to Log Analytics / Event Hub / Sentinel. Together these policies protect against lateral movement, sovereign-data exfiltration, and audit gaps in regulated workloads. Maps to NIS2 Articles 21–23, ISO 27001 A.13.1.3 (network segregation) and A.18 (compliance), NIST SP 800-207 (Zero Trust).
