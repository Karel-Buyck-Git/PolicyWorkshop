# Managed Application Policies

## Tier rationaleHardened

**Essential** — Baseline hygiene for Managed Application: the non-negotiable controls every deployment should enforce from day one. This tier protects against credential theft, unencrypted data exposure, and accidental data loss for Managed Application workloads. Maps to CIS Benchmarks, ISO 27001 Annex A.10 (cryptography) and A.12 (operations).

**Professional** — No professional-tier policies are defined for Managed Application in the current built-in policy set.

**Enterprise** — Zero-trust and regulatory alignment for Managed Application: controls that require infrastructure investment or map directly to compliance frameworks. This tier delivers direct mapping to regulatory framework controls. Together these policies protect against lateral movement, sovereign-data exfiltration, and audit gaps in regulated workloads. Maps to NIS2 Articles 21–23, ISO 27001 A.13.1.3 (network segregation) and A.18 (compliance), NIST SP 800-207 (Zero Trust).
