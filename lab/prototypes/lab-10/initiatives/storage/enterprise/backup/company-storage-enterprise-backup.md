# Company Storage Enterprise — Backup

## Tier rationale

**Enterprise** — Zero-trust and regulatory alignment for Backup: controls that require infrastructure investment or map directly to compliance frameworks. This tier delivers private endpoints and private link removing the public attack surface, diagnostic settings streaming to Log Analytics / Event Hub / Sentinel, customer-managed keys (CMK / BYOK) for cryptographic sovereignty, zone-redundant deployments backing the 99.99% SLA, and direct mapping to regulatory framework controls. Together these policies protect against lateral movement, sovereign-data exfiltration, and audit gaps in regulated workloads. Maps to NIS2 Articles 21–23, ISO 27001 A.13.1.3 (network segregation) and A.18 (compliance), NIST SP 800-207 (Zero Trust).
