# Site Recovery Policies

## Tier rationale

**Essential** — No essential-tier policies are defined for Site Recovery in the current built-in policy set.

**Professional** — No professional-tier policies are defined for Site Recovery in the current built-in policy set.

**Enterprise** — Zero-trust and regulatory alignment for Site Recovery: controls that require infrastructure investment or map directly to compliance frameworks. This tier delivers private endpoints and private link removing the public attack surface. Together these policies protect against lateral movement, sovereign-data exfiltration, and audit gaps in regulated workloads. Maps to NIS2 Articles 21–23, ISO 27001 A.13.1.3 (network segregation) and A.18 (compliance), NIST SP 800-207 (Zero Trust).
