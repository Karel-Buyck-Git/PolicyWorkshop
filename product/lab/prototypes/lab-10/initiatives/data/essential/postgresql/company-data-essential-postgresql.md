# Company Data Essential — PostgreSQL

## Tier rationale

**Essential** — Baseline hygiene for PostgreSQL: the non-negotiable controls every deployment should enforce from day one. This tier delivers TLS / HTTPS enforcement preventing in-transit interception, encryption-at-rest with service-managed keys, and backup, soft-delete, and purge-protection guards against accidental or malicious data loss. Together these policies protect against credential theft, unencrypted data exposure, and accidental data loss. Maps to CIS Benchmarks, ISO 27001 Annex A.10 (cryptography) and A.12 (operations).
